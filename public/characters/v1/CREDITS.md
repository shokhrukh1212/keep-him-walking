# Character asset credits

These are review candidates, not approved replacements for the published traveler.
Asset acquisition cost: **$0**. No Mixamo or other third-party motion recordings
are included. Animation clips were authored for this project in Blender.

## MakeHuman Community — CC0

Human base mesh, identity morphs, bundled expression units, young skin textures,
eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, `braid01` and `shoes05` come from MakeHuman
Community / MPFB system assets. The system-asset headers credit Data Collection
AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Body proportions, materials, clothing
visibility and skeletal animation were modified for these characters.

## Project-authored additions

Backpack, watch/prop/seat geometry where present, animation
curves, character assembly and runtime code were authored for Keep Him Walking.
Upstream component licenses above continue to apply to the combined models.

Blender 4.5.4 LTS and MPFB 2.0.17 are authoring tools; their executables are not
included in the website bundle. The editable `.blend` sources include packed
textures and the animation actions.
