# Draft notice to Dodo Payments — changed sponsorship proposal (16 September 2026)

Subject: Keep Him Walking — updated sponsorship proposal (checkout remains disabled)

Hello Dodo Payments team,

I'm writing about the merchant review for Keep Him Walking (https://keephimwalking.com),
operated by Shokhrukh Karimov. The sponsorship offer I described earlier has changed, and I want
the review to be based on the current proposal.

**What changed**
- The earlier offer (one sponsor for a fixed seven-day season at USD 499) is withdrawn. It was
  never sold: no sponsorship requests, payments or webhooks were ever processed.
- The site now runs a free, fourteen-day virtual journey (17–30 September 2026). Watching and
  voting are free, and payments never influence any vote.

**The new proposal (not live)**
- One featured sponsor at a time, shown as a disclosed placement on the website. The proposed
  starting price is USD 50.
- A new sponsor could replace the current one by paying twice the current sponsor's price
  (USD 50 → 100 → 200 → 400…). The displaced sponsor would receive a full refund, and the
  replacement would get the remaining travel period.
- Every sponsor's material would be manually reviewed before publication under the published
  Content and Listing Moderation Policy.

**Current state of the site**
- Checkout is disabled and shows "Checkout unavailable — Awaiting payment-provider approval."
- The site accepts no bids, requests, payments or reservations. Interested sponsors can only
  message me on X.
- Separately, a Buy Me a Coffee link accepts voluntary support. It is processed by Buy Me a
  Coffee and does not buy a sponsor slot, a placement or a vote.

**Please confirm** whether this replacement model (a sponsor displaced with a full refund,
priced by doubling) is acceptable under your policies. If it is not, tell me which part needs to
change. I will not enable checkout until you approve it, and I will publish the full
sponsor terms and refund steps before accepting any payment.

Public pages: /sponsors, /sponsor-terms, /refund-policy, /terms, /privacy, /content-moderation.

Thank you,
Shokhrukh Karimov
