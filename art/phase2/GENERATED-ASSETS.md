# Phase 2 generated asset record

Generated on 2026-09-02 with the repository `imagegen` workflow. The original PNG masters are retained under each city’s `zones/`, `props/`, and `npc/` folders. `scripts/process-phase2-art.mjs` creates deterministic runtime WebP/WAV derivatives.

Environment prompt template: premium 2D side-scrolling animated travel-film environment; painterly editorial realism; subtle canvas texture; sophisticated natural light; coherent eye-level wide composition; sky/distance above, locally distinctive architecture/vegetation in the middle, unobstructed walking ground below; no people, close vehicles, lettering, logos, collage, repeated buildings, or seams. Each of the five prompts then named the exact route zone and its landmark, street/market/food details, vegetation, weather, and time of day.

Prop prompt template: transparent production sheet with exactly three isolated same-baseline cutouts—local mature tree, local lamp/directional detail without lettering, and local flower planter—using the city’s materials and restrained regional ornament.

NPC prompt template: transparent production sheet with one respectful contemporary local adult host in exactly three consistent full-body poses—neutral, speaking, reacting—with the same proportions, clothing, baseline, and painterly environment style. The prompts explicitly prohibited tourist costume and stereotypes.

Tashkent v4 promotes the repaired Phase 1.5 source direction into the v3 schema and new immutable runtime paths. Dushanbe, Bishkek, Almaty, Baku, Tbilisi, and Istanbul use newly generated city- and zone-specific masters; no cross-country environment image is recolored or reused.
